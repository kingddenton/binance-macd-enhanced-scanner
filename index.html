4H change = (latest price − reference price) ÷ reference price × 100. Reference price is the close of the latest completed 1-minute candle at or before the timestamp four hours ago, so the reference can be up to one minute older than exactly four hours. 24H change is Binance's rolling 24-hour ticker value.
