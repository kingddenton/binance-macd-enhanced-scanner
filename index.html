The early MACD crossover uses an adjustable baseline candle count (default 10). This count applies only to the early-crossover engine. Additional MACD/DEA, RSI and Supertrend confirmations are evaluated independently on every selected timeframe and do not use the early-crossover candle count. Selected confirmation timeframes use AND logic: every selected condition must agree with the bullish or bearish crossover. MACD uses standard 12, 26, 9 settings.
