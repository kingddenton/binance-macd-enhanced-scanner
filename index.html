The MACD histogram crossover candle is Bar 1. Signals remain eligible through the selected crossover window and disappear after that window. Only completed candles are used for calculations and confirmation. DEA confirmation uses the selected DEA timeframe: positive DEA confirms bullish signals; negative DEA confirms bearish signals. A DEA value of exactly zero confirms neither. DIF and DEA zero-line filters are independent optional filters and also use the selected DEA timeframe. The table displays MACD timeframe Histogram and DEA values. RSI and Supertrend each have independent timeframe selections. MACD uses standard 12, 26, 9 settings.
